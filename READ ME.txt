Some items are not randomized to prevent softlocks (But transition randomizers can still softlock you)
Bloody Key
Old Kings Bonedust
Big boi cake and smol bean drink

Also transitions may feel odd but they are (mostly) reversible as in A to B goes B to A
But some doors function in an odd manner causing it not to be reversible the same way, rabbit hole and the ward are good examples

Randomized items in shops will cost 3x their sell value or in cases where there is no sell value will cost the same as the item they replace
